ChangeIS: Project Delivery Case Studies

A practical record of how complex systems were improved, stabilized, or rebuilt: using disciplined engineering, clear trade-offs, and measurable outcomes.

What this is

This site is a curated set of real-world project case studies delivered across financial services, security, infrastructure, and regulated environments.

Each case study focuses on what changed, why it mattered, and what was learned: not on tools for their own sake.

How to read the case studies

Every case study is documented using the S.T.A.R.R.Y.© format. This connects decisions and delivery with evidence, lessons learned, and business value. The guiding aim is simple: Extend your reach.

SSituationThe business or operational context, the problem, and why it mattered.
TTasksThe objectives, responsibilities, and constraints of the work.
AActionsThe concrete steps taken, including design, implementation, and collaboration.
RResultsWhat changed, supported by evidence such as response time, reliability, or quality.
RReflectionWhat was learned, what worked, and what could be improved or repeated.
YYieldThe value produced: financial return, operational capacity, risk reduction, or strategic benefit. State estimates and their basis clearly.
